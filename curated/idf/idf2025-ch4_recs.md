# Capitolo 4 - Blood glucose-lowering therapies - insulin options for type 2 diabetes - raccomandazioni


## Insulin initiation

**Optimal Care**
- Begin insulin therapy when optimised glucose-lowering medications and lifestyle interventions do not maintain target blood glucose control

**Basic Care**
- Begin insulin therapy when optimised available glucose- lowering medications and lifestyle interventions do not maintain target blood glucose control

## Support

**Optimal Care**
- Provide education, continuing lifestyle management, and regular review, including telehealth

**Basic Care**
- Provide education, continuing lifestyle management, and regular review

## Monitoring

**Optimal Care**
- Self-monitoring of blood glucose
- Consider CGM with complex insulin regimens and insulin pump therapy
- Regular HbA1c measurement

**Basic Care**
- Self-monitoring of blood glucose
- Regular HbA1c measurement

## Insulin regimen

**Optimal Care**
- Select from a broad range of affordable insulins and insulin delivery devices
- Access to insulin pump therapy available

**Basic Care**
- Select from a limited range of affordable and continuously available insulin

## Insulin initiation regimens

**Optimal Care**
- Commence with single daily injection of basal analogue insulin
- Continue metformin (if tolerated and not contraindicated)
- Continue with other blood glucose- lowering therapies if appropriate

**Basic Care**
- Commence with single daily injection of affordable human, analogue, or biosimilar insulin
- Continue metformin (if tolerated and not contraindicated)
- Continue with other affordable and available blood glucose-lowering therapies if appropriate

## Insulin intensification

**Optimal Care**
- Options include:
  - Basal bolus
  - Premixed insulin
  - Add GLP-1RA (either separately or switch to FRC)
  - Insulin pump

**Basic Care**
- Options include:
  - Basal bolus
  - Premixed human insulin
  - Other affordable and available blood glucose-lowering therapies

## Insulin deintensification

**Optimal Care e Basic Care (indicazione unica)**
- Regularly review insulin dose, need for insulin, and complex insulin regimens, especially in the elderly and individuals at target glycaemia or experiencing hypoglycaemia
- Deintensify and simplify treatment as required
