# Capitolo 6 - Cardio-renal protection in type 2 diabetes - raccomandazioni


## Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease (ASCVD) - Screening/risk assessment

**Optimal Care e Basic Care (indicazione unica)**
- History of ASCVD
- High-risk assessment
  - Age plus 2 risk factors
  - CVD risk score

## Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease (ASCVD) - Management

**Optimal Care**
- Glycaemic control
- Risk factor control
- Blood pressure/lipid control
- Consider aspirin
- SGLT2i or GLP-1RA
- Consider SGLT2i and GLP-1RA

**Basic Care**
- Glycaemic control
- Risk factor control
- Blood pressure/lipid control
- Consider aspirin
- SGLT2i*

## Heart Failure (HF) - Screening/risk assessment

**Optimal Care**
- Symptoms
- Hospitalisation for HF
- ECG
- Echocardiography
- Risk score
- Biomarkers
  - BNP / NT-proBNP

**Basic Care**
- Symptoms
- Hospitalisation for HF
- ECG
- Risk score

## Heart Failure (HF) - Management

**Optimal Care**
- Glycaemic control
- Risk factor control
- Blood pressure/lipid control
- ACEi or ARB
- Beta-blockers
- Metformin+SGLT2i
- Consider adding a non-steroidal MRA
- Consider adding GLP-1RA/tirzepatide in obese individuals

**Basic Care**
- Glycaemic control
- Risk factor control
- Blood pressure/lipid control
- ACEi or ARB
- Beta-blockers
- Metformin+SGLT2i*
- Consider adding a steroidal MRA

## Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) - Screening/risk assessment

**Optimal Care e Basic Care (indicazione unica)**
- eGFR
- UACR

## Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) - Management

**Optimal Care**
- Glycaemic control
- Risk factor control
- Blood pressure/lipid control
- ACEi or ARB
- SGLT2i
- Consider adding semaglutide

**Basic Care**
- Glycaemic control
- Risk factor control
- Blood pressure/lipid control
- ACEi or ARB
- SGLT2i*
