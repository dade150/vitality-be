# Capitolo 5 - Weight control in type 2 diabetes mellitus management - raccomandazioni


## Weight reduction

**Optimal Care**
- Lifestyle change
- VLCD
- Incretin-based therapy
- Metabolic bariatric surgery

**Basic Care**
- Lifestyle change
- Low-calorie diet
- Use weight-sparing BGL therapy
- SGLT2i if available

## Improving diabetes control

**Optimal Care**
- Lifestyle change
- Incretin-based therapy
- Metabolic bariatric surgery

**Basic Care**
- Lifestyle change
- Metformin
- Add weight-sparing BGL therapy when indicated
- SGLT2i if available

## Diabetes prevention

**Optimal Care**
- Lifestyle change
- Consider:
  - Metformin
  - SGLT2i/incretin-based therapy
  - Metabolic bariatric surgery

**Basic Care**
- Lifestyle change
- Consider metformin

## Diabetes remission

**Optimal Care**
- Lifestyle change
- VLCD
- Metabolic bariatric surgery

**Basic Care**
- Lifestyle change
- Low-calorie diet
- VLCD
